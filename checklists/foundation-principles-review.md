# Foundation Principles Review Checklist

- **Checklist ID:** UIXS-FP-CHECK
- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **Status:** Draft
- **Related standard:** `references/00-foundation-principles.md`

## Applicability preflight

- [ ] Review scope, users, tasks, risk, and decisions are stated.
- [ ] Relevant principles are selected and exclusions are explained.
- [ ] One primary topic rule owns each finding; related FP rules are linked without duplication.
- [ ] Evidence and assumptions are identified.

## Principles

- [ ] **FP-001:** Every major decision maps to a user need, task, decision, or risk.
- [ ] **FP-002:** Meaning, state, consequence, and next action are clear.
- [ ] **FP-003:** Correctness, safety, accessibility, and outcomes precede aesthetics or engagement.
- [ ] **FP-004:** Essential information and operation do not depend on one sensory or input mode.
- [ ] **FP-005:** Hierarchy follows user purpose, consequence, and risk.
- [ ] **FP-006:** Complexity is staged without hiding cost, risk, consent, uncertainty, or controls.
- [ ] **FP-007:** Context, options, instructions, and history are visible at the point of need.
- [ ] **FP-008:** Equivalent roles are consistent and intentional variants are justified.
- [ ] **FP-009:** Consequential actions provide control, cancellation, confirmation, or recovery.
- [ ] **FP-010:** Predictable errors are prevented and valid input survives correction.
- [ ] **FP-011:** Loading, progress, success, failure, empty, stale, offline, and permission states are communicated.
- [ ] **FP-012:** Metrics, scales, denominators, uncertainty, missing data, source, freshness, and time context are honest.
- [ ] **FP-013:** No dark pattern, coerced consent, hidden consequence, false urgency, or obstructed refusal exists.
- [ ] **FP-014:** Important assumptions and high-risk decisions have evidence and validation plans.

## Conflict and conformance

- [ ] Conflicts follow the documented precedence order.
- [ ] Unsatisfied MUST rules use the approved exception process.
- [ ] Automatic Fail conditions were checked.
- [ ] Every finding includes evidence, severity, primary rule, related principles, correction, owner, and verification.
- [ ] Critical and unresolved Major findings prevent Pass.
