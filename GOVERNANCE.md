# Governance

## Document states

- **Draft** — under discussion.
- **Review** — ready for structured review.
- **Candidate** — approved for validation.
- **Stable** — approved for normal use.
- **Deprecated** — retained for history but not for new work.

## Versioning

UIXSenseee uses semantic versioning:

- MAJOR — incompatible normative changes.
- MINOR — new rules or standard areas.
- PATCH — clarification without changing normative meaning.

## Rule IDs

Prefixes: `FP`, `IA`, `VF`, `IN`, `CB`, `DV`, `AX`, `RS`, `CT`, `EP`, and `QA`. Use three digits, for example `IA-001`. Published IDs are permanent.

## Policy precedence

Safety → rights/privacy/control → accessibility → task success → honest information → clarity → consistency → efficiency → aesthetics.

## Exception policy

A normative exception must include evidence, affected users, risks, mitigation, owner, and review date. Convenience alone is not sufficient.

## Approval

A standard can move from Draft to Review only when its scope, terminology, normative rules, validation methods, exceptions, anti-patterns, and acceptance checklist are complete.
